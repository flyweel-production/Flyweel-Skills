# Platform benchmarks

Rough ranges. Vertical, country, funnel stage and creative move them a long way. Use them only when the account has no history of its own, and say that you are doing so.

| Platform | Click rate | Cost per click | Cost per 1,000 impressions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search | 3–6% | $1–5 (legal, finance and insurance $5–50) | n/a |
| Google Display / Performance Max | 0.3–1% | $0.30–2 | $2–10 |
| Meta (Facebook / Instagram) | 0.8–2% | $0.50–3 | $5–15 |
| LinkedIn | 0.4–0.8% | $5–15 | $30–80 |
| TikTok | 0.5–1.5% | $0.30–2 | $3–10 |

Conversion rate from click to lead: Google Search 3–8%, Meta 1–5%.

## Metric definitions

| Metric | Formula |
|---|---|
| Click rate (CTR) | Clicks / impressions |
| Cost per click (CPC) | Spend / clicks |
| Cost per 1,000 impressions (CPM) | Spend / impressions × 1,000 |
| Conversion rate | Results / clicks |
| Cost per result (CPA) | Spend / results |
| Return on ad spend (ROAS) | Revenue / spend |
| Frequency | Impressions / reach |
| Impression share (Google) | Impressions won / impressions eligible |
